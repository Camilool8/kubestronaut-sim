**The project has demonstrated broad adoption, contributors from multiple organizations, and mature governance** is correct: graduation is the highest CNCF maturity level. To graduate, a project must show sustained production adoption, a healthy contributor base that is not dependent on a single company, well-defined governance, and security practices, as assessed by the CNCF Technical Oversight Committee. Kubernetes, Prometheus, and Envoy are well-known graduated projects.

Why the others are wrong:

- **The project has been accepted into the CNCF and is in its earliest experimental stage** — that describes the sandbox level, the entry point for early-stage projects, not graduation.
- **The project is now maintained exclusively by CNCF staff rather than by its community** — the CNCF provides a neutral home and support services, but projects are always developed and maintained by their own open source communities, never taken over by foundation staff.
- **The project has been merged into the Kubernetes codebase and follows its release cycle** — CNCF maturity levels are independent of Kubernetes. Graduated projects remain separate projects with their own codebases and release cadences.
