**The Linux Foundation** is correct: the CNCF is a foundation hosted by the Linux Foundation, a nonprofit organization that provides shared legal, financial, and administrative infrastructure for open source projects and foundations. This is why CNCF projects are governed by neutral, vendor-independent processes rather than by any single company.

Why the others are wrong:

- **The Apache Software Foundation** — the ASF is a separate, independent nonprofit that hosts its own portfolio of open source projects (Apache Kafka, Apache Spark, and others); it has no organizational relationship to the CNCF.
- **Google** — Google donated the original Kubernetes codebase to seed the CNCF and remains an active contributor and member, but the CNCF is a vendor-neutral foundation, not a Google-operated body.
- **The Open Source Initiative (OSI)** — the OSI is the organization that reviews and approves open source LICENSES (like Apache 2.0 or MIT); it does not host or govern projects the way the CNCF does.
