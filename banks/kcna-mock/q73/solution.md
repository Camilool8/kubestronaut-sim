**Kubernetes** is correct: Google open-sourced Kubernetes (its container-orchestration system, informed by internal systems like Borg) and donated it to the newly formed CNCF in 2015, making it the foundation's founding project. Kubernetes later became the first CNCF project to reach the "graduated" maturity level.

Why the others are wrong:

- **Prometheus** — Prometheus (originally built at SoundCloud) was the SECOND project accepted into the CNCF, in 2016, and was also the second to graduate; it was not the founding donation.
- **Envoy** — Envoy, created at Lyft, joined the CNCF in 2017, well after the foundation's founding project was already established.
- **containerd** — containerd was donated by Docker in 2017 as the CNCF was already an established foundation with several hosted projects; it was not part of the CNCF's 2015 launch.
