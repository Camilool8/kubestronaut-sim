**To map and catalog the cloud native ecosystem's projects and products by category, helping users discover and compare options** is correct: the Landscape is an interactive, continuously updated visual map — organized into areas like orchestration, observability, runtime, and app definition — that helps practitioners find relevant tools and understand how they relate to one another, including both CNCF-hosted projects and the wider commercial and open source ecosystem around them.

Why the others are wrong:

- **An official, ranked list of which tools are certified as production-ready** — inclusion in the Landscape is not a certification or an endorsement of readiness; it is a discovery map, and a project's actual maturity is instead expressed through the separate sandbox/incubating/graduated system.
- **A fixed reference architecture every CNCF project must implement** — the Landscape catalogs what already exists across many different architectural approaches; it does not prescribe one required architecture for projects to follow.
- **A billing and cost-comparison tool for cloud providers** — the Landscape has no pricing or billing function; it is organized around technical categories, not cost.
